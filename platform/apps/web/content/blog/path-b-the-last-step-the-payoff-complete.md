## Path B — the last step + the payoff *(complete)*

### Path B — you + an AI marketing agent

5. You open one review queue: approve, tweak, or send it back. One pass, one place — and nothing went out the door without that pass.

**Five steps. Four of them are the agent working; one is you deciding.** The case study sits in your CMS as a draft, publish held. The three posts sit queued, send held. The newsletter section sits in the next issue, held. Every irreversible move waits on your yes.

### The count we promised

Same Tuesday job, same three deliverables — a published case study, three platform-native posts, a newsletter section.

- **Path A — you + ChatGPT:** 11 steps. You did 8 of them. ChatGPT wrote three drafts; you were the glue for everything else — the digging, the fact-checking, the formatting, the platform rewrites, the queuing, the trimming.
- **Path B — you + an AI marketing agent:** 5 steps. You did 2 of them — pointing it at the win, and approving the result. The agent did the middle three.

The point isn't "fewer steps." It's *which* steps stay yours. In Path B the two that are yours are the only two that should be: aiming the work, and deciding what ships. The eight in the middle — the glue work — stop being yours. (We counted them. We said we would.)
