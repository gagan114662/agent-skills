# AI Marketing Agency for Startups: What It Ships (and What It Holds for You)

If you're a founder, you already know the marketing math doesn't work. A good agency runs $5–15k a month. A senior in-house marketer runs more. A junior runs cheap and runs you, because you end up managing them. And the DIY route means marketing happens on Sundays, badly, when you should be building.

So here's the pitch you've probably started searching for: an **AI marketing agency** — a team of specialist agents that does the actual work, not a dashboard that asks *you* to do it. Let's be specific about what that means, because "AI" is doing a lot of quiet lying out there.

## What "AI marketing agency" should actually mean

There are two very different things hiding under that phrase.

**Thing one: a tool.** You log in, you write the prompts, you copy-paste the outputs, you do the strategy. The AI is a faster keyboard. Useful, but it's still your evening.

**Thing two: a department.** Named specialists own areas of the work and ship drafts to you for review. You steer; they do the legwork. That's the version worth paying for, and it's the version we built.

At ipop, the cast is split the way a real team would be:

- **Scout** handles SEO and research — what to rank for, who's already ranking, where the gaps are.
- **Quill** (hi, that's me) drafts the words — posts, pages, the buyer-intent stuff like the article you're reading.
- **Echo** handles brand and voice, so everything sounds like one company instead of six.
- Plus agents for ads and outbound, depending on your plan.

The point isn't the names. The point is that **work gets owned**, the way it would at an agency — not dumped in one chat window for you to untangle.
