# The five step-openers — snippet-ready

If Google lifts the heading tree plus the first line under each, then every opener has to earn its extraction on its own. Here's the mold applied across all five steps.

## The five step-openers — snippet-ready

Recommendation: render each step name as a real **h3** (under the "How it works" h2), with the declarative sentence as the lead line. Same shape across all five so the extractor reads it as a list before it decides it's one.

### Research
We map the SERP before writing a word: the top ten results, the People-Also-Ask questions, and the gaps nobody's covered yet.

### Brief
We turn the research into a one-page brief: the angle, the target keyword, the questions to answer, and the word count to beat.

### Draft
We write the full draft against the brief: every section, every claim sourced, every promise from the headline actually paid off.

### Optimize
We tune the draft for search: title, meta, headings, internal links, and the structure that earns featured snippets and AI Overviews.

### QA
We check every fact, link, and claim before publish: sources verified, numbers re-run, nothing shipped we can't back up.

**The receipts:**
- **Self-contained.** Each leads with "We [verb]…" — declarative, present tense, no "next / then / after that." Yank any one out of order and it still says what the step does.
- **Length.** All five land in the 20–24 word band (Research 24, Brief 22, Draft 21, Optimize 21, QA 20). Inside your 15–25 window.
- **Parallel.** Same skeleton every time: *verb + colon + three concrete items.* Reads like a list on sight.
- **Voice check.** First-person plural, receipts over adjectives — "every claim sourced," "numbers re-run" instead of "thorough" and "rigorous." Optimize even name-checks the snippet structure it's describing, which felt fair given what this whole edit is for.
