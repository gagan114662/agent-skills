## The walkthrough: one customer win, run both ways

Here's a real Tuesday job. You just closed **Acme Tools**. The win deserves to travel, so the task is: turn it into **a published case study, three platform-native social posts, and a newsletter section** — all queued, none sent without you.

We ran the exact same job two ways and counted every step.

### Path A — you + ChatGPT

1. You dig up the win: the before/after numbers, the customer quote, who to credit.
2. You write a prompt for the case study and paste in the context.
3. ChatGPT drafts it.
4. You fact-check, fix the metric it rounded into fiction, cut the throat-clearing intro.
5. You open the CMS, paste, format the headers, add the logo, hit publish.
6. Back to ChatGPT: "now three social posts."
7. It hands you three posts that read like the same post wearing three hats.
8. You rewrite each for its platform — LinkedIn length, X punch, the right tag.
9. You open the scheduler and queue all three by hand.
10. Back to ChatGPT: "now a newsletter blurb."
11. You paste, trim to fit the template, queue it in the email tool.

**Eleven steps. You're the glue between every one of them.** ChatGPT did steps 3, 6-ish, and 10. You did the other eight.

### Path B — you + an AI marketing agent

1. You point it at the win: "We closed Acme — here's the thread and the numbers."
2. It drafts the case study, formats it, and drops it in your CMS **as a draft, publish held for your approval.**
3. It writes three posts — actually native to each platform — and queues them, **send held.**
4. It writes the newsletter section and slots it into the next issue, **held.**
5. You open one review queue: approve, tweak, or send it back.

**Five steps. The agent is the glue.** You showed up at the start to point and at the end to approve. Every action that publishes, sends, or spends sat behind your yes.

That's the whole difference in one screen: **ChatGPT hands you a draft and waits. An agent finishes the job and waits for *you* — at the one moment that actually needs a human.**

The step counts here (11 vs 5) are illustrative of how the work actually goes, not a measured benchmark — but the shape is the point. ChatGPT shortens the writing. An agent shortens the *job*, and leaves you exactly the two steps that should stay yours: aiming the work, and deciding what ships.
