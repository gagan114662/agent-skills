# AI Marketing Agency vs. Hiring a Marketer: How to Decide as a Founder

You've got the spreadsheet open. One row says "first marketing hire." Another says "try the AI thing." Both of them are asking you to bet money you'd rather spend on the product. Let's make the call together, with numbers instead of vibes.

## The honest version of the trade-off

Hiring your first marketer is a real commitment: a salary, a ramp, a job description you're not sure how to write because you've never done the job yourself. An AI marketing agency is a different shape of bet — lower up front, faster to start, but it's newer and you're right to be skeptical of anything that promises the moon.

So we won't promise the moon. Here's how the two actually compare on the things a founder cares about.

| | First marketing hire | AI marketing department |
|---|---|---|
| **Time to first work** | Weeks to hire, then weeks to ramp | Same day |
| **Cost to start** | Full salary from day one | Free to start, no card |
| **Coverage** | One person, one skillset | Multiple specialists at once |
| **Judgment & taste** | Human, accountable, in the room | Drafts and plans; you're the one who ships |
| **Off hours** | They sleep (good!) | Drafts around the clock |

The row that matters most is the last two together. A human marketer has taste and accountability you can't fully replace. AI agents have range and stamina a single hire can't match. The question isn't which one is "better" — it's which gap is hurting you right now.

## When to hire the human

Hire first if:

- You already know exactly what marketing you need, and it's deep in one channel (a paid-ads specialist, say) rather than broad.
- You want someone *accountable* in meetings, owning the number.
- You have the runway to absorb a ramp that doesn't pay off for a quarter or two.

A great first marketer is worth it. We're not here to talk you out of one.

## When the AI department fits better

Reach for the agents if:

- You need *several* kinds of marketing work at once — SEO research, brand polish, content, ads, outbound — and can't hire five people.
- You want to see real work shipped before you commit budget.
- You're the bottleneck: ideas are fine, execution time is the problem.
