### An AI marketing agency for startups: what you actually get

You're a founder. Marketing is the thing that keeps sliding to next week. You've got four options on the table, and they all have a catch:

- **Hire in-house.** $90k+ and three months to fill the seat — for one person who can't be great at SEO *and* ads *and* writing.
- **Hire an agency.** Good ones are expensive and booked; the affordable ones hand you a junior and a slide deck.
- **Freelancers.** You become the project manager you hired someone to avoid being.
- **Do it yourself.** It's free, the way a second job is free.

An **AI marketing agency** is the fifth option. Here's the honest version of what that means — what ships, what you steer, and where it fits.

### What an AI marketing agency actually is

It's a team of specialist agents that each own a lane, the way a real department does. At ipop, that's Scout on SEO and research, Quill on content, Echo on the brand voice, and the rest of the department on ads and outbound. You don't prompt a chatbot and paste the output around. You set the direction; the department does the legwork and brings you work to approve.

The difference that matters: it runs around the clock and doesn't context-switch. While you're shipping product, Scout is mapping what your buyers actually search, Quill is drafting against it, and Echo is keeping it all sounding like you instead of like everyone.

### What you actually get, week one

Not "AI-powered insights." Actual output:

- A keyword and content plan built from what your buyers search — not a word cloud.
- Drafts you can read, edit, and ship — blog posts, landing copy, outbound — in your voice.
- Brand and competitor research with the sources attached, so you can check the work.
- Ad and campaign plans laid out before a dollar moves.

You'll have things to approve on day one, not a 30-day "onboarding."

### Where a human stays in the loop

This is the part most "autonomous" tools get quietly wrong, so we'll be loud about it: **every irreversible or money move waits for you.** Publishing, sending, spending — those are held for a human click. The agents draft and plan at full speed; you keep the keys to anything you can't take back. You steer, they ship. In that order.
