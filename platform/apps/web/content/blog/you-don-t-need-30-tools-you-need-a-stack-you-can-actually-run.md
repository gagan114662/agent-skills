## You don't need 30 tools. You need a stack you can actually run.

Every list ranking the "best AI marketing tools" has the same shape: thirty-something logos, a feature grid, an affiliate link, and a paragraph each. [Marketer Milk lists 30](https://www.marketermilk.com/blog/ai-marketing-tools). [cs-cart lists 37](https://www.cs-cart.com/blog/ai-marketing-tools/). [Canto](https://www.canto.com/blog/best-ai-marketing-tools/) and [Zapier's 17](https://zapier.com/blog/best-ai-marketing-tools/) fill in the rest. All useful if your problem is "I haven't heard of enough software."

That's not your problem. If you're a founder or a team of three with nobody whose job is marketing, your constraint isn't *which tool* — it's that every tool ships you a blank screen and a monthly bill, and someone still has to sit down and operate the thing.

Here's the stat the long lists bury: [88% of marketers use AI daily, but only 38% have actually integrated it into how they work](https://www.averi.ai/how-to/best-ai-marketing-tools-for-startups-in-2026-(ranked-compared)). The gap between "I have the tool open" and "the work is getting done" is the whole game. So this list is organized by the job you need done, not by logo. One or two honest picks each, with what they cost.

## Organize by job-to-be-done, not by logo

**Content — blog posts, emails, landing copy**
The category everyone starts with, because it's the one a blank page punishes most. [Jasper runs around $69/seat/month](https://www.marketermilk.com/blog/ai-marketing-tools) and is built for teams that publish on a schedule. If you're earlier and just need to stop staring at the cursor, a general assistant (ChatGPT/Claude at ~$20/mo) does 80% of it. *Honest caveat: the tool drafts; it doesn't decide what's worth saying. That part's still you.*

**Ads & creative — the visual stuff you can't design yourself**
This is where a tiny team bleeds time. [Creatify runs ~$19/month](https://www.marketermilk.com/blog/ai-marketing-tools) for AI video ads from a product URL — cheap enough to test before you've raised. Pair it with a design tool you already pay for rather than adding a second seat.

**SEO & visibility — getting found**
The job here isn't "generate 50 articles," it's "figure out what's actually worth ranking for and whether you can." Most startups over-buy this. Start with one keyword/brief tool, not a suite.

**Social scheduling — showing up without babysitting a calendar**
A scheduler is the highest-leverage cheap purchase on this list, because the work is repetitive and the tool genuinely removes it. Mid-double-digits a month, and you stop forgetting to post.

**Automation glue — making the above talk to each other**
Zapier or similar. The unsung pick: it's what turns five disconnected tools into something resembling a workflow. This is usually the difference between the 88% and the 38%.

## What it actually costs

Add up one honest pick per job and you're looking at [roughly $100–300/month for two or three tools that earn their keep](https://www.averi.ai/blog/what-ai-marketing) — not the $1,500/mo a fourteen-logo stack would run you. The trap isn't spending too little. It's buying eight tools, integrating none, and paying for the privilege of eight blank screens.
