You searched "best AI marketing tools for startups" and got a 30-tool spreadsheet ranked, roughly, by who pays the highest affiliate commission. Helpful if you enjoy spreadsheets. Less helpful if you have eleven other fires and a runway that doesn't care how many tools *exist*.

## The rule before the stack: one job, one tool, then go

Pre-PMF, every tool you add is a tool you have to *operate*. The goal isn't coverage — it's the shortest path to "we published a thing and learned something." So the stack below is sequenced. Buy #1. Get it working. Only then look at #2.

## 1. The writing-and-publishing tool (buy this first)

**What it replaces:** the blank page, and the contractor you'd hire to fill it.

Before PMF, your highest-leverage marketing motion is *saying what you do, clearly, in public, repeatedly.* That's blog posts, landing copy, launch announcements, the occasional sales email. An AI writing tool that's actually good at drafting in your voice collapses the "I'll write that this weekend" backlog that every founder is carrying.

What to look for: drafts in *your* voice (not generic SaaS mush), keeps a brief, and lets a human approve before anything goes live. `[verify representative tools + current pricing]`

Why it's #1: it touches every other channel. Ads need copy. SEO needs posts. Outbound needs sequences. Start at the source.

## 2. The SEO / research tool (buy this second)

**What it replaces:** guessing what your buyers actually type into Google.

Once you're producing copy, you want to point it somewhere with intent behind it. An SEO tool tells you which questions your buyers are searching, how hard each is to rank for, and what page-one already looks like — so you write the post that *answers the question the listicles dodge* instead of the 31st version of one that exists.
