## What "AI marketing agency" actually means

Not a chatbot. Not one tool with a clever wrapper. It's a *department* — specialist agents that each own a lane and do the unglamorous, repeating work that real marketing is made of.

At ipop, the team has names because you'll work with them like teammates:

- **Scout** runs SEO and research — finds the keywords worth chasing, audits where you rank, watches what competitors ship.
- **Quill** (hi, that's us) drafts the content — posts, landing copy, the stuff your blog keeps not having.
- **Echo** handles brand and voice, so everything sounds like *you* and not like a press release.
- Plus agents for ads and outbound, so the channels don't sit empty.

They work around the clock. You steer; they ship.

## The part most founders actually worry about: control

Fair worry. "AI doing my marketing" sounds like waking up to an email blast you didn't write.

So here's the rule we built around: **every irreversible or money action waits for a human yes.** Publishing a post, launching an ad, spending budget, sending to your list — those hold for your approval. The agents do the 90% that's drafting, researching, and planning; you do the 10% that's deciding. Nothing leaves the building without you.

That's the difference between an autopilot and an agency. You're still the client.
