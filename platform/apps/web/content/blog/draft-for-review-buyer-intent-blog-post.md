### The honest version of the pitch

You're a founder with more roadmap than runway, and marketing is the plate that keeps wobbling. The options all have a catch:

- **Hire a marketer** — months to find, expensive to keep, and one person can't be a strategist, writer, SEO nerd, and ads buyer at once.
- **Use an agency** — good work, slow loops, retainer that hurts before the results land.
- **DIY with a stack of tools** — now *you're* the bottleneck, stitching together ten dashboards at 11pm.

An AI marketing agency is a fourth option, and we'd rather tell you where it fits than oversell it. So here's the straight version.

### What an AI marketing agency actually is

It's a fleet of specialist agents that do the marketing work — not a chatbot, not another dashboard you have to drive. At ipop, the department has roles, the same way a real one does:

- **Scout** runs SEO and research — keywords, competitor teardowns, content gaps.
- **Quill** (hi, that's the writer) drafts posts, landing copy, and emails.
- **Lens** keeps everything on-brand so it sounds like *you*, not like a robot wearing your logo.
- **Echo** and the rest handle ads and outbound.

They work around the clock and hand you the output. You steer; they ship. (Made by robots, steered by humans — we put that on the homepage because we mean it.)

### The part most "AI marketing" pages skip: where the human stays in charge

Here's the line we won't cross. Every irreversible or money-spending action — publishing live, sending an outbound sequence, pushing ad spend — is **held for your approval**. The agents draft, plan, and queue. You hit go.

That's not a limitation we're embarrassed by; it's the design. A startup can't afford an autonomous system that "helpfully" emails 4,000 prospects or burns the ad budget overnight. So we don't build that. You get the speed of automation with a human hand on the last lever.

### Does the math work for a startup?

The reason this category exists for startups specifically: the cost structure flips.

- A full-time marketing hire is a salary plus the months it takes to find them.
- An agency retainer bills whether or not this month produced anything.
- An AI marketing department starts **free, no card** — you pick a paid plan when the work has paid for itself, and you can change or cancel any time.

That last part matters more than it sounds. You're not making a bet on a hire or signing a retainer on faith. You see the drafts, the research, the plans — *then* you decide what it's worth.
