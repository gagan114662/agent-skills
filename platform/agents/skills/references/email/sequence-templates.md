# Lifecycle Email Sequences

One email = one goal = one CTA. If an email has two asks, it has zero. Every send is triggered by a user *action* (or inaction), never a calendar blast.

## Welcome Sequence (4 emails) — fires on signup confirmation
The most-opened mail a brand ever sends is the welcome (~50–60% open). Spend it.

1. **The Deliver** — *Trigger: email confirmed.* Goal: deliver what they signed up for *now* (the lead magnet, the login, the first value), set the from-name relationship. Subject angle: plain/personal — "Here's your [thing], [name]". Structure: one warm line, the asset/link above the fold, what to expect next. CTA: *Open / claim the thing.*
2. **The Origin** — *Trigger: +1 day.* Goal: earn trust via the why, not the what. Subject angle: curiosity — "The reason we built this". Structure: short founder-voice story → the problem you remove → social proof line. CTA: *Read one customer story.*
3. **The Quick Win** — *Trigger: +3 days.* Goal: get one tangible result fast so the product sticks. Subject angle: specific-value — "Do this in 5 minutes, see X". Structure: numbered 3-step path to first value. CTA: *Complete step 1.*
4. **The Ask** — *Trigger: +5 days.* Goal: the soft conversion or the segmentation question. Subject angle: question — "Which of these is you?". Structure: 2–3 clickable segments that route them. CTA: *Pick your path.*

## Onboarding / Activation Sequence — fires on account-created, gated by behavior
Goal of the *whole* sequence: drive the one "aha" action that predicts retention (the activation event). Send the next email only if the prior step is incomplete.

1. **Setup Nudge** — *Trigger: account created, activation event NOT done in 24h.* Goal: complete the single setup step. Subject angle: specific-value — "You're one step from [outcome]". Structure: progress bar, the single missing step, a 60-second how-to. CTA: *Finish setup.*
2. **Use-Case Bridge** — *Trigger: setup done, no core action in 48h.* Goal: connect setup to *their* job-to-be-done. Subject angle: question. Structure: "Most teams start with X" → one templated starting point. CTA: *Use this template.*
3. **Activation Celebrate** — *Trigger: activation event fires.* Goal: reinforce + expand. Subject angle: plain — "Nice — you just [did the thing]". Structure: praise → the natural next feature → tip. CTA: *Do the next thing.*

## Trial-Conversion Sequence — fires on trial start, day-aware
Map to the trial length. For a 14-day trial:

1. **Value Recap** — *Trigger: day 7.* Goal: surface what they've already gotten. Subject angle: number — "You saved 4 hours this week". Structure: their usage stats (real data, never fabricated) → what paid unlocks. CTA: *See your plan.*
2. **Objection Handler** — *Trigger: day 11.* Goal: remove the #1 blocker. Subject angle: question — "Worried about [common objection]?". Structure: name the fear → the answer → a guarantee/proof. CTA: *Upgrade.*
3. **Deadline** — *Trigger: day 13 (24h left).* Goal: scarcity that's real. Subject angle: plain-urgent — "Your trial ends tomorrow". Structure: what they lose at expiry → one-click keep-access. CTA: *Keep my access.*
4. **The Door's Open** — *Trigger: day 16 (post-expiry).* Goal: recover the lapsed. Subject angle: personal — "Want more time?". Structure: offer a short extension or a discount. CTA: *Reactivate.*

## Win-Back / Re-Engagement Sequence — fires on inactivity threshold (e.g. 60–90 days no open/login)
Protect deliverability: this is also a *sunset* gate — non-responders get suppressed.

1. **We Miss You** — *Trigger: 60 days inactive.* Goal: re-open the relationship. Subject angle: curiosity — "Did we do something wrong?". Structure: short, human, "here's what's new since you left" (2 bullets). CTA: *See what's new.*
2. **The Incentive** — *Trigger: no engagement +5 days.* Goal: a reason to come back. Subject angle: specific-value — "[X]% off to pick up where you left off". Structure: the offer + expiry. CTA: *Claim it.*
3. **Permission Pass / Goodbye** — *Trigger: still no engagement +5 days.* Goal: confirm consent or cleanly exit. Subject angle: plain — "Should we stop emailing you?". Structure: "Click to stay subscribed — otherwise we'll stop." CTA: *Keep me subscribed.* No click ⇒ suppress (protects sender reputation; see deliverability).
