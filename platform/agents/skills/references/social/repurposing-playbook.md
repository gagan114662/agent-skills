# Repurposing Playbook

Repurposing is not reposting. Reposting shows the same idea twice and trains the audience to scroll past you. Repurposing takes one *insight* and refracts it through five different *angles* — each post stands alone, each rewards a different reader, and nobody notices they share a core.

## The five angles (one idea → five posts)

Take one insight — e.g. "Niche content outperforms broad content." Now rotate it:

1. **Story angle** — the moment you learned it. "We had 12k followers and zero leads. Then we narrowed to one persona and pipeline tripled in a quarter." Lead with scene and stakes.
2. **Contrarian angle** — the belief it overturns. "'Reach more people' is why your content isn't converting. The fix is to reach *fewer*, on purpose."
3. **How-to angle** — the repeatable method. "How to niche down without losing your audience: 5 steps." Numbered, skimmable, actionable.
4. **Proof angle** — the data/result. "Broad posts: 0.4% reply rate. Niched posts to one persona: 3.1%. Same effort, 7x the conversation." Numbers do the talking.
5. **Question angle** — provoke the audience to self-diagnose. "Quick gut check: can you name the one person every post is for? If not, that's the leak." Invites replies, which feeds reach.

That's a full week from a single insight, with zero repetition. Rotate the *angle*, hold the *idea*.

## Content pillars

Pick 3–5 pillars — recurring themes the audience associates with you (e.g. *acquisition tactics*, *founder lessons*, *teardowns*, *behind-the-scenes*). Every post belongs to a pillar. Pillars give you (a) a topic generator when you're stuck, (b) a recognizable identity, and (c) a balance check — if 80% of posts are one pillar, you're a one-note account. Aim for a rough rotation so the feed stays varied but coherent.

## Blog post → a week of social (concrete workflow)

Start with one 1,500-word blog post. Mine it like ore:

**1. Extract the spine.** Pull the 1 core thesis + 3–5 supporting points + every stat, example, and quotable line. This list is your raw material.

**2. Build the X thread (5–9 tweets).**
- Tweet 1 = the thesis as a hook (use a hook-library pattern — specific number or contrarian works best for threads).
- Tweets 2–7 = one supporting point each, compressed to a single sharp claim + one line of support.
- Final tweet = recap + CTA ("Full write-up linked in the next reply / bookmark this"). Drop the blog link in a reply, never the post.

**3. Build the LinkedIn post.** Don't paste the thread. Reframe as one narrative: hook in the first two lines (before "see more"), the thesis as a story or lesson, the supporting points as a whitespaced list, a single question CTA. Link in first comment. Sweet spot 1,200–1,600 chars.

**4. Carve 3 standalone tweets.** Each takes ONE idea from the post and stands completely alone — no thread, no link needed:
- a one-stat tweet (the most surprising number),
- a one-line contrarian claim (the thesis stripped to a provocation),
- a one-tip tweet (the single most actionable takeaway).

Schedule these across the days the thread *isn't* running so the well-spaced cadence holds.

**One blog post → 1 thread + 1 LinkedIn post + 3 tweets = ~6 assets, a full week, zero duplication.** Then start the next blog post — but first, check which angle *resonated*, and lead the next batch with that.
