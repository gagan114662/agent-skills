# The first customer of ipop is ipop

Every customer story page should open with a confession, so here's ours: **we didn't hire an agency to
write this page. A content agent named Quill drafted it, and a human approved it before it went live.**
The same is true of the comparison pages, the SEO guides, and the changelog you'll find on this site.
This is the dogfood story — the product, demonstrating itself.

> "We figured the most honest marketing site we could build was one the fleet built itself. If the
> agents can't run our own marketing, why would we ask you to trust them with yours?"

## The setup

ipop ships as a marketing **department**, not a single assistant: seven named specialists, one channel
each — Scout on SEO, Quill on content, Echo on social, Postmark on email, Bid on ads, Lens on
analytics, Mark on brand. They draft; a human approves anything that leaves the building. Behind that is
a venture loop that scores ideas for fundability, an approval gate on every outbound send, and a
self-healing loop that turns failures into fixes.

## What the fleet actually did here

- **Scout** read this site the way a search crawler does and flagged the pages missing meta descriptions
  and structured headings.
- **Quill** drafted these comparison pages and this story in the house voice — warm, plural, receipts
  over adjectives.
- **Echo** summarised a week of merged pull requests into the changelog you can read on `/changelog`.
- **A human** approved every publish. Nothing on this site went live without that yes.

## The numbers, as of launch

These are the real shape of the loop that runs this site — not vanity metrics, the mechanical ones we
can actually point at:

- **7** specialist agents, each scoped to draft-only tools — none of them can send, post, or spend.
- **100%** of outbound publishes routed through the #13 approval gate. There is no "auto-publish" path.
- **3** comparison pages, **2** cornerstone guides, **1** auto-drafted changelog — all committed as
  reviewable markdown, all authored by the fleet.
- **0** lines of this content hand-written by a marketer on retainer.

## The honest caveat

This is early. The fleet doesn't have press relationships, it won't sit across a table from you, and a
human still steers every decision that matters. We think that's the right division of labour — agents do
the volume, you keep the judgement — and this site is the proof we're willing to eat our own cooking.
