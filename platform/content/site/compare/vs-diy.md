# ipop vs. doing it yourself

You already do your own marketing. You write the posts, you ship the landing page, you stare at the
analytics at midnight. And honestly? For a while, that's exactly right. Founder-led marketing has a
voice nothing else can fake. This page is about the moment that stops being true.

## Where DIY wins

- **Your voice is the real thing.** Early on, customers want to hear from *you*, not a brand. Don't
  outsource that — keep it.
- **You have the most context.** You know why the product exists, who it's for, and what almost killed
  it. That context is gold and it lives in your head.
- **Zero coordination cost.** No briefing, no review queue. You think it, you ship it.

## Where it stops scaling

- **You are one person with one calendar.** SEO audits, a week of social, three email variants, a
  comparison page — each one is a night you don't get back.
- **The boring-but-important work slips.** Meta descriptions, alt text, a consistent posting cadence.
  Not hard, just relentless. It's the first thing to fall off when you're shipping product.
- **You stop testing.** When every experiment costs *your* hours, you run fewer of them. That's the
  silent tax of DIY.

## What changes with a fleet

You keep the voice and the judgement. You hand off the volume. You brief an agent the way you'd brief a
teammate: audit the homepage for SEO, draft a comparison page, or turn a customer objection into a guide.
The work shows up in the channel for you to approve. The fleet drafts; **you still decide what ships**.

| | Doing it yourself | ipop |
|---|---|---|
| Authentic founder voice | **Yes** | You steer it; agents match it |
| Hours per week | All of them | A few, mostly approving |
| Experiments you'll actually run | The ones you have time for | As many as you'll review |
| The boring SEO work | Slips | Gets done |
| Cost | Your time | A flat plan |

## The honest version

If you're at the stage where founder-led marketing is your edge — stay DIY a little longer. The day you
notice the important-but-boring work piling up, or you catch yourself *not* running an experiment
because you're tired, that's the signal. Keep the voice. Delegate the volume.

Start free. Your first agent is waiting for a brief.
