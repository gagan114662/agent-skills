# ipop vs. hiring a marketing agency

Let's be straight: a good agency is a real thing. If you've got the budget and you want a team of
senior humans who've shipped a hundred launches, an agency can be worth every dollar. This page isn't
here to tell you agencies are bad. It's here to help you tell **which one your stage actually needs** —
and to be honest about where each one falls down.

## Where an agency wins

- **Senior judgement on big bets.** A rebrand, a category-defining campaign, a Super Bowl spot — that's
  human craft. We're not going to pretend otherwise.
- **Relationships.** Press contacts, influencer rolodexes, the warm intro to a publication. Agencies
  carry those; software doesn't.
- **Accountability with a face.** Someone to sit across the table from when a launch slips.

## Where an always-on fleet wins

- **It never sleeps and never context-switches.** Your agency juggles a dozen clients. Your fleet works
  one account: yours. Brief an agent at 11pm and the draft is waiting at 11:01.
- **The marginal cost of "one more idea" is zero.** A/B five subject lines, draft three landing pages,
  audit every page for SEO — there's no change order, no scoped hours, no awkward "that's extra."
- **Everything is in one channel, in the open.** You watch the work happen — research, drafts, plans —
  instead of waiting for a Friday status deck.
- **You approve everything that leaves the building.** Nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your
  explicit yes. The agents draft; you ship the good ones.

## The honest trade-off

| | Hiring an agency | ipop |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to first draft | Days to weeks | Minutes |
| Cost structure | Retainer + scoped hours | Flat monthly plan |
| Availability | Business hours, shared | Always on, dedicated |
| Senior human judgement | **Yes** | You bring it (you steer) |
| Press & relationships | **Yes** | Not yet |
| Approval control | Varies | Every send is gated |

## So which one?

If you're pre-product-market-fit and you need volume, speed, and a tight feedback loop without burning a
retainer — start with the fleet. If you're scaling a brand and need senior humans on a six-figure bet —
an agency earns its keep. Plenty of teams do both: the fleet handles the daily grind, the agency handles
the moonshots.
