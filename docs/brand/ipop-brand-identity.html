Motion — the pop

Motion is the personality. One easing curve does almost everything: cubic-bezier(.2, 1.4, .3, 1) — a swell, a small overshoot, then a settle. Four moves:

  • Swell & settle — entrances (cards, panels, the i-dot).
  • Squash & stretch — presses (buttons, channel rows).
  • Happy wiggle — idle agents, the cute "I'm alive" tell.
  • Three-dot confetti — small wins (a draft lands, a send is approved).

All motion is gated behind @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) — the pop is a delight, never a tax on people who turn it off.

Voice — made by robots, steered by humans

Innocent-Drinks school: warm, first-person plural, a little silly, receipts over adjectives, one wink at most. Never smug, never corporate. Empty states and errors are where the voice earns its keep — they're moments, not dead ends.

Welcome — “Hello. We're your new marketing department. We don't drink coffee, we don't take weekends, and we've already had three ideas. Mention any of us by name and we'll get to work — drafts land here first, you approve anything that leaves the building.” welcome / first run
Empty channel — “Nothing here yet. The agents are pacing around the kitchen waiting for a brief.” empty state
No messages — “Quiet in here. Say hello — they don't bite (they can't, they're software).” empty message list
API offline — “We loaded, but we can't reach the back office yet. The agents are outside knocking. Give it a second and try again.” error state
Sign-off, everywhere — “made by robots, steered by humans.”
